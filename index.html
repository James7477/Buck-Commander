☎ CALL 911 — US / Canada

This opens the phone’s calling interface; you must complete the call. The browser cannot play a reliable automatic announcement to dispatch. For other countries, use the local emergency number.

✉ Text 911 (where supported)

Call if you can, text if you can't. Text-to-911 is not available everywhere. Paste the message, send it yourself, and wait for a dispatcher reply. Do not assume your text went through.

🚑 Nearby help

Searches Google Maps near the emergency coordinates if available; otherwise your selected map location. Results are not verified or necessarily staffed. In an emergency use the local emergency number, not a search listing.

📻 Radio backup

US CB channel 9 is reserved for emergency communications and traveler assistance. There may be no one monitoring it. Emergency services typically use separate public-safety radio systems; the app cannot identify a reliable local dispatch channel. Check your radio type and local radio regulations before transmitting.
